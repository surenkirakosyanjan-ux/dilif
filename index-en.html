About the Village

Dilif (Georgian: დილიფი) is a village in the Ninotsminda Municipality of the Samtskhe-Javakheti region of Georgia. It is located on the Javakheti plateau, at an altitude of more than 1,900 meters above sea level. The village stands out with its beautiful nature, clean air, and hospitable residents.

History and Culture

The village has a rich history and cultural heritage. The population is mainly engaged in animal husbandry, agriculture (potato cultivation), and beekeeping. The traditional holidays and customs of Javakheti have been preserved in Dilif from generation to generation.

Sightseeing

Saint Vardanants Church

The Saint Vardanants Church of Dilif village was built in 1866. However, over the years it fell into disrepair and was turned into a warehouse for storing various types of grain.

Carved on the wall of the church entrance is: "1866 Saint Vardanants Church. Dilif village". In the records of 1847-1885, until 1870, the active church in the village is mentioned under the name of Saint George, and afterwards as Saint Vardan. In the 1880s, the church under the name of Saint Vardan is mentioned as a stone structure with a wooden roof.

The church also had its own parish school, information about which is reported starting from the 60s of the 19th century. According to these, it was opened through the funds of the local community in 1864 and had 20 students at the time.

In 2017, initiated by former residents of Dilif, church restoration works were carried out. The roof of the church was repaired, the dome was restored, and interior finishing works were completed.

⛪ On October 12, 2018, by the hand of His Grace Bishop Vazgen Mirzakhanyan, Primate of the Armenian Diocese in Georgia, the Holy Vardan Church was re-consecrated. The door-opening ceremony was performed accompanied by Priest Tigran Mkhitaryan and Priest Gevorg Asatryan.

Nature of Javakheti

Surrounding mountains and alpine meadows that offer a unique view in any season of the year.

🏞️ Natural uniqueness: The high mountains and alpine meadows of Javakheti are cool and green in summer, and covered with a thick layer of snow in winter, creating fairy-tale views.

🌾 Local nature: The surroundings of the village are rich in clean mountain air, medicinal herbs, and natural crystal springs that give life to local agriculture and animal husbandry.

Springs and Monuments

The cool springs of the village and the monuments dedicated to local heroes and history.

💧 Cool springs: Dilif is famous for its cold and crystal springs, which for centuries have been the gathering place for villagers and a source of coolness in the summer heat.

🎖️ Memory and respect: The monuments erected in the village symbolize the heroic past, patriotism, and respect of the people of Dilif for the memory of their ancestors who gave their lives for the homeland.

Dilif Public School

The school is the most important educational and cultural center of the village, where the children of Dilif study.

🏆 Page of Pride: School student Sergey Avetisyan (9th grade) won the "Etalon" intellectual competition and represented the Ninotsminda Municipality in the final round.
